Questions file ↗
Saved on this device. To feed the daily robot, drop them into your questions file — tap “Copy all”, then paste there. Every morning at 9am it researches each one and adds it to the Path, opening new chapters and modules when a topic earns it.

The daily loop

  1. Read a card. When a question lands, tap “Ask about this” on the card — or jot it here.
  2. Each day, those questions get researched properly — origins, commentary, the honest debates.
  3. A new card (or a whole chapter) is written from your question and added to the Path.
  4. The site updates itself. The course follows your curiosity instead of a fixed syllabus.

On honesty

Where scholars disagree — how old the texts are, where the Vedic people came from, what a contested verse means — the cards say so plainly, and give the mainstream view alongside the traditional one.

Add to your phone
